01ガイド

The Guide

Most lost items in Japan are recoverable — if you act before the holding deadline. Contact the place where you lost it, ask whether they're holding it, how long they'll keep it, and whether they can ship it to you. Everything else follows from those answers.

1–3 months

Hotels, hostels and ryokan usually keep lost property this long. Small properties may clear items sooner.

7 days

Railways move fast. Stations hold items for a few days; unclaimed items are handed to the police within about a week.

3 months

Police hold lost property for three months from the public notice. After that, recovery is effectively over.

a.Left it at a hotel→The most recoverable case. The desk has it logged — the usual sticking point is that they can only ship within Japan. b.Left it on a train→Time-critical. Each railway has its own lost-and-found desk, and items move to a central office, then to the police. c.Left it at an airport→Two desks: the terminal for public areas, the airline for anything left on board. Contact both. d.It has gone to the police→Different rules. File a Lost Property Report and work to the three-month deadline. Advice is free. e.The full step-by-step guide→Contact details, deadlines and the complete procedure for hotels, trains, airports and the police.

Once you are back home, three things are usually out of reach: a Japanese address the facility can ship to, a Japanese speaker to deal with the facility, and someone who forwards parcels from Japan. This service provides all three.

If the facility can ship it straight to your home, we'll tell you — and that's the end of it. No charge.

One total, before you pay.

If the item is found and the facility can only ship inside Japan, we give you one all-in total before you pay: the $80 service fee, handling and any pickup needed, and the shipping — domestic and international. We don't add anything later.

06よくある質問

Questions

Is the check really free?

Yes. Contacting one facility, up to two attempts, costs nothing, and there is nothing to pay if the item is not found, cannot be released, or the facility can ship it to you directly. A second facility, or further follow-ups, are quoted before any work is done.

What if the facility doesn't reply?

We contact the facility up to two times. If there is still no reply, the free check ends there — we tell you what we tried, and there is nothing to pay.

How long do I have?

It depends on where you left it. Hotels usually hold items for one to three months. Trains are urgent — items move to the police within about a week. Police hold items for three months from the public notice. Ask about the deadline first; it decides what can still be done.

How long does it take?

The check depends on the facility's reply — it can take a few days, sometimes longer, and if there is no answer after two attempts we tell you and the free check ends. Once an item reaches us, forwarding time depends on the destination and the service: post takes days to weeks, a courier is faster. In the Ireland case, DHL estimated about three days.

My item is with the police. Can you collect it?

No — and we will not charge you for trying. Police release usually has to happen in person or by domestic mail, with a signed letter of authorisation. What we do for free is explain how to file a Lost Property Report and where your item is most likely being held.

What can't you handle?

Passports and official IDs — report those to your embassy and the holding police station. Cash, perishable food, and dangerous goods are also out. Battery items are checked case by case, because carriers restrict them.

How do I pay?

Only after you have seen the total and agreed to it — never before. On Fiverr, the platform holds your payment until the order is complete. If you contact us directly, we send you a PayPal link for the agreed total.

What if my item is lost or damaged on the way?

We pack items carefully, photograph them before shipping, and send everything with tracking, by post or courier. If a parcel is lost or damaged in transit, we help you file the carrier's claim. Compensation follows the carrier's terms and the value declared on the shipment, so we confirm that value with you before shipping. What we cannot do is guarantee the carrier's decision.

Why can't the hotel just ship it to me?

Usually three practical reasons: they would have to sign a customs declaration for someone else's belongings, international postage cannot be collected on delivery, and their whole system is set up for domestic returns only.
